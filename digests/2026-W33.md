2.  **Over-complication/Under-simplification of Tools:** Users struggle with finding the "just right" tool. Enterprise-grade tools like Jira are often seen as overkill for smaller teams or startups due to their complexity, overhead, and need for dedicated administrators (Review 14). There's a clear demand for flexibility and structure in a single tool (Review 4).
3.  **Manual, Repetitive, and Time-Consuming Tasks:** PMs are spending excessive time on manual tasks, particularly in spreadsheet tracking for status updates, action items, risks, and financial elements like change orders and job costing (Reviews 1, 11, 24). This directly contributes to stress and impacts efficiency, especially in high-volume environments.
4.  **Ineffective Communication & Visibility:** Despite using multiple tools, there's a recurring issue with status updates that aren't read, critical risks being missed, and a general lack of clear, actionable information flow (Reviews 1, 6). The disconnect between operational staff and management due to lack of visibility is also highlighted (Review 5).
