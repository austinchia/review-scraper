*   **Maintainability, Error Handling, and Production Readiness of AI/No-Code Apps:** A recurring concern is the "silent failure" of automations, lack of robust error handling, and the difficulty in assessing if an AI-built or no-code app is truly "production ready" for real users. Questions arise about long-term maintainability, the quality of AI-generated code, and the need for human oversight (audits) to prevent issues after launch.
